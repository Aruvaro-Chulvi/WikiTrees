*Version 1 — 2026-11-20.* When you send a tree to WikiTrees you accept this agreement by ticking its box in
the form (or in the pull request). · *Versión 1 — 2026-11-20.* Al enviar un árbol a WikiTrees aceptas este
acuerdo marcando su casilla en el formulario (o en el pull request).

WikiTrees and of the Offlined project (https://www.offlined.org).

1. **Authorship.** You keep the authorship of your tree and you will be credited with the name you give

2. **Public licence.** You agree that your tree is published in WikiTrees under the same licence as the rest
   of the trees, [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE): anyone may use, share and adapt it for **non-commercial**
   purposes, crediting you.
3. **Grant to Offlined.** In addition, you grant the maintainer — and, through the maintainer, the Offlined application
   and the people and companies that develop and distribute it — a **worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
   perpetual and irrevocable** licence to use, reproduce, adapt, translate, correct, combine with other
   trees, distribute and make available your tree, **also for commercial purposes**, and to sublicense these
   rights to third parties who distribute Offlined. This is what allows the tree to be included in Offlined.
4. **Your rights to send it.** You confirm that you made the tree yourself (with or without the help of an
   AI assistant, whose result you have reviewed) or that you otherwise have the right to grant the above, and
   that the cover image, if any, is yours or you may share it under these terms. A tree contains only titles
   of things and of Wikipedia articles, and links to them; do not include text copied from other works.
5. **Review and publication.** The maintainer decides freely whether to publish a tree, may correct it
   (fix links, titles or translations) and may stop distributing it at any time. Nobody is paid for trees.
6. **Withdrawal.** You may ask for your tree to stop being distributed in WikiTrees and Offlined from then
   on; copies already distributed and the licences in points 2 and 3 for them are not affected.
7. **No warranty.** The tree is provided as is.

WikiTrees y del proyecto Offlined (https://www.offlined.org).

4. **Tus derechos para enviarlo.** Confirmas que has hecho el árbol tú (con o sin ayuda de un asistente de IA,
   cuyo resultado has revisado) o que tienes derecho a conceder lo anterior, y que la imagen de portada, si
   la hay, es tuya o puedes compartirla en estas condiciones. Un árbol solo contiene nombres de cosas y de
   artículos de Wikipedia, y enlaces a ellos; no incluyas texto copiado de otras obras.
5. **Revisión y publicación.** El responsable decide libremente si publica un árbol, puede corregirlo
   (enlaces, títulos o traducciones) y puede dejar de distribuirlo en cualquier momento. Nadie cobra por los

6. **Retirada.** Puedes pedir que tu árbol deje de distribuirse en WikiTrees y Offlined a partir de ese
   momento; las copias ya distribuidas y las licencias de los puntos 2 y 3 sobre ellas no se ven afectadas.
7. **Sin garantía.** El árbol se ofrece tal cual.
